Rules FAQ

Any client modification that reveals ores, aims, or clicks for you is a permanent removal — no warnings, no refunds on ranks.

Unclaimed land is fair game at any hour. Inside a claim it’s a ban — raid weekend or not.

Five minutes of safety around spawn. Greeting new players with a hole full of lava ends your run.

No slurs, no real-world politics, no targeted harassment. Trash talk about the game is fine; about people, it isn’t.

Found an exploit? Report it in Discord and keep the finder’s fee. Duped items get wiped retroactively, whoever holds them.

AFK farms are allowed until they lag the server. Staff will ask you to trim it once before trimming it for you.

Bad trades are on you. Duplicating items, clipping through walls, or farming server crashes are on us.

Argue later, argue in the appeals channel. Pleading a case in global chat just earns you a mute.
